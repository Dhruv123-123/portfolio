Cooking

I lost my grandma in 2021 to Alzheimer's. Cooking was the one thing we always shared — I grew up next to her at the stove, and long after the disease took most everything else, her recipes stayed. She left behind a cookbook full of them.

This summer I started a journey to make every single one. This page is the record — each dish as I get to it, her recipe, and how mine came out.
