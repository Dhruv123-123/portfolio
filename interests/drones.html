Drones

Drones · 2024–2025

Background

I built my first quadcopter from a kit and a lot of soldering in 2024, mostly to figure out how something that unstable manages to hover. The answer is a control loop running a few hundred times a second, and once I understood that the hobby stopped being about flying and started being about the software.

Teaching it

Through the MathWorks campus ambassador program at Berkeley I ended up running a hands-on workshop where students built drone flight paths in Simulink and then watched real quads fly them. Turnout was up about forty percent from the previous workshops, which I'd love to say was my teaching but was honestly because the drones were real. Nobody shows up to a workshop about block diagrams. Everybody shows up when something takes off.

Now

I don't fly much anymore, but the drones were the first time I saw a model I wrote control something physical, and that line from a number on a screen to a thing moving in the real world is the same one I followed into robotics and physical AI. It started with a quad hovering unevenly in a Berkeley parking lot.
