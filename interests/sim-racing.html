F1 and sim racing

Sim racing · since 2020

Background

I got into F1 in 2020 like a lot of people did, during lockdown, when there was nothing else on and Drive to Survive was right there. I started watching the races properly after that and pretty quickly I wanted to actually drive the cars instead of just watching them, and sim racing is the only realistic way to do that.

I started out in the F1 game on a controller, which works but you're basically fighting the game the whole time. A few months in I got my first wheel, a Thrustmaster T150. I still remember setting it up the first day and doing a bunch of laps at like half pace just because the force feedback felt so different from anything I'd used before. That was the point where it went from a game to a hobby.

League racing

Public lobbies are a mess. Somebody always dive bombs you into turn one and then quits. So I joined an online league, which is a fixed grid of the same people every week, real qualifying, stewards who go back and review incidents and give out penalties, and a championship that runs for a whole season. It's way more fun and also way more stressful, because you actually know the people you're racing and you're going to see them again next week.

Most of my time in the sim ended up being at Monza, honestly. It's the track I know best and it's the one where I'd just do laps for hours trying to shave off tenths.

Top 800

At my peak I was ranked in the top 800 in the world in ranked play. What I learned from getting there is that being fast for one lap doesn't matter that much. The people ahead of me weren't really faster than me over one lap, they just made fewer mistakes over a full race. So I started actually looking at telemetry and overlaying my laps against faster people, and it turns out I'd been braking too early into slow corners for like a year without knowing.

Now

I still race most weekends and I still watch every race. The league has slowed down a lot since everyone started working, but the group chat is still going. At some point I want to do a real karting league or a track day and see how much of this actually carries over. I think the braking will and the bravery won't.
