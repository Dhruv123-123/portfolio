F1 and sim racing

F1 got me in. I watched every race, then wanted to know what it actually felt like to drive one, and sim racing was the closest I could get without a budget of several hundred million dollars.

It stopped being casual fast. I raced in online league racing, with proper qualifying, stewards, and championships that ran for months, and at my peak I was ranked in the top 800 in the world.

It's also the hobby that taught me the most about consistency: a fast lap is worth nothing if you can't do it forty times in a row.
