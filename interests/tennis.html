Tennis

Tennis · 2014–2022

Background

Tennis was the first thing I was ever actually serious about. I started at eight at a club in Bengaluru with cracked hard courts and a coach who thought running was a moral virtue, and for most of my childhood I was on court five days a week before school. I played junior tournaments around Karnataka and for a while I was genuinely decent.

The forehand and the other thing

I had one weapon, a heavy topspin forehand, and I built my whole game around never having to hit a backhand. My coach spent eight years trying to fix this and did not succeed. I'd run around the ball to the point of absurdity, and once opponents figured that out, which the good ones did fast, my tournament was over.

Why I stopped

Two things, the cube and the pandemic. Cubing took over my free time around fourteen, and then 2020 closed every court in the city for most of a year. By the time they reopened I'd gotten into running, which needs no court, no partner, and no backhand. I played my last tournament at sixteen and didn't miss it as much as I thought I would.

Now

I still hit occasionally and the forehand still works. Tennis gave me the thing every sport since has relied on, which is being able to lose on a Saturday morning in front of your parents and show up again on Sunday.
