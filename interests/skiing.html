Skiing

Skiing · winters since 2018

Background

I learned to ski at twelve on a family trip to Gulmarg in Kashmir, which is one of the most beautiful places on earth and an insane place to learn, because the mountain does not have a gentle side. I spent three days falling down it and came home certain I'd do it every winter for the rest of my life. So far, so good.

Tahoe

Being in the Bay Area means Tahoe is three hours away, or five in traffic, or nine in a storm. I go most winters, usually to Northstar or Palisades, usually on a day that starts at 5am and ends with me asleep in the passenger seat. I ski blues with confidence and blacks with regret, and I've made peace with that ratio.

Conclusion

The goal is to get comfortable off-piste before my knees have an opinion about it. Realistically, the goal is to keep going every winter, because a day on a mountain with no signal fixes something that nothing else does.
