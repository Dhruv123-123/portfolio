Skiing

Skiing · winters since 2018

Background

I learned to ski when I was twelve on a family trip to Gulmarg in Kashmir, which is one of the most beautiful places on earth and a crazy place to learn because the mountain doesn't really have an easy side. I spent three days falling down it and came home sure I'd do this every winter for the rest of my life. So far that's held up.

Tahoe

Being in the Bay means Tahoe is three hours away, or five in traffic, or nine if it's snowing. I go most winters, usually Northstar or Palisades, usually on a day that starts at 5am and ends with me asleep in the passenger seat. I'm comfortable on blues and I do blacks with some regret, and I've made peace with that.

Now

I'd like to get comfortable off piste before my knees start having opinions about it. Realistically the goal is just to keep going every winter, because a day on a mountain with no signal fixes something nothing else does.
