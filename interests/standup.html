Standup comedy

Standup · 2023–2024

For about a year I did standup. Not well and not for long, but I did it. Nine open mics across Berkeley and SF, five minutes each, in rooms where the audience was mostly other comics waiting for their own five minutes and not really listening to yours.

The first one was probably the worst thing I've ever voluntarily done. I forgot a joke, replaced it with silence, and got one laugh, from a guy in the back who I'm pretty sure was laughing at his phone. The second was almost as bad. Somewhere around the fifth, a bit about growing up cubing actually landed, and I got why people chase that feeling for decades in rooms that smell like beer.

Two of the nine sets were good. I have recordings of all nine. You might be noticing a pattern with me and recordings.

Partly time once the startup stuff got serious. Mostly because I'd learned what I wanted to learn, which is how to stand in front of strangers who don't care and try to make them care, and be fine either way. That carries over to everything, demos, interviews, pitching. Nothing has really scared me since.
