Standup comedy

Standup · 2023–2024, retired

Background

For about a year I did standup. Not well, and not for long, but I did it: nine open mics across Berkeley and San Francisco, five minutes each, in rooms where the audience was mostly other comics waiting for their own five minutes and not listening to yours.

What it was like

The first one was the worst thing I have ever voluntarily done. I forgot a joke, replaced it with silence, and got one laugh, from a man at the back who I'm fairly sure was laughing at something on his phone. The second was nearly as bad. Somewhere around the fifth, a bit about growing up cubing landed, actually landed, and I understood why people chase that feeling for decades in rooms that smell like beer.

Two of the nine sets were good. I have recordings of all nine. You may notice a pattern with me and recordings.

Why I stopped

Partly time, once the startup work got serious. Mostly because I learned what I wanted to learn: how to stand in front of strangers who don't care and make them care, or fail to, and survive either way. That transfers to everything. Demos, interviews, pitching. Nothing has scared me since.

Conclusion

Retired at nine mics. Undefeated, if you don't count the seven bad ones.
