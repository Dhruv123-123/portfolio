Aviation

I'm fascinated by the world of aviation accidents. Not the spectacle, but the investigations: the chain of small, individually survivable failures that line up into a disaster, and the way every one of them changed how planes are built and flown afterward.

I think there's still a lot of safety work to be done there, and a lot of it is a data problem. A few months ago I vibecoded a tool that builds a knowledge graph of every crash on record, linking aircraft, causes, airlines, and the recommendations that came out of each one. I find it pretty cool.
