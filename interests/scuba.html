Scuba diving

Scuba · certified 2022

I got my PADI Open Water certification in 2022 on Havelock Island in the Andamans, which is the kind of place that ruins every other beach for you. The pool sessions were boring. The first real descent, about twelve meters down onto a reef with visibility you could measure in bus lengths, was one of the best hours of my life.

Diving is the only thing I've done where you genuinely cannot be anywhere else in your head. You're breathing, you're watching your gauge, you're watching your buddy, you're watching a turtle that does not care about you at all. No phone, no sound except your own breathing, and a strong reason to stay calm. For someone who can't stop thinking it's the closest thing to an off switch I've found.

I've done a dozen or so dives since, most recently in Monterey, where the water is cold and green and the kelp forests feel like flying through a cathedral. I want to do my Advanced cert. Mostly I want to go back to the Andamans.
