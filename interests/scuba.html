Scuba diving

Scuba · certified 2022

Background

I got my PADI Open Water certification in 2022 on Havelock Island in the Andamans, which is the kind of place that makes you resent every other beach you've ever been to. The pool sessions were tedious. The first real descent, twelve meters down onto a reef with visibility you could measure in bus lengths, was one of the two or three best hours of my life.

Why it stuck with me

Diving is the only thing I've done where you cannot be anywhere else in your head. You are breathing, you are watching your gauge, you are watching your buddy, you are watching a turtle that does not care about you at all. There's no phone, no sound except your own breath, and a strong incentive to stay calm. For someone who can't stop thinking, it's the closest thing to an off switch I've found.

Conclusion

I've done a dozen dives since, most recently in Monterey, where the water is cold and green and the kelp forests make it feel like flying through a cathedral. I'd like to do my Advanced certification. Mostly I'd like to go back to the Andamans.
