Guitar

Guitar · started 2026

This is the newest one. I listen to mostly rock and I've been getting more into metal over the last year, and at some point in early 2026 I decided I wanted to actually be able to play the stuff I was listening to instead of just hearing it. So I got a brand new Yamaha Pacifica and a Fender Mustang LT25, and that's what I've been practicing on.

Nobody warns you about the calluses. My fingertips hurt for about two weeks straight and I couldn't type properly, which is a problem when typing is your job. Then one morning they stopped hurting and the strings stopped buzzing and it started being fun.

I'm learning the way everyone learns now, a YouTube course for the basics and tabs for the songs. The F chord took me eleven days. I counted.

Right now I'm learning Everlong by the Foo Fighters. The intro is fine, the drop D thing took a bit to get used to, and the fast strumming in the verses is where I'm at. After that the plan is to keep working through the songs that made me want to play in the first place, roughly in the order I can physically manage them.

Most of the stuff I do I've been doing for years and I'm at least okay at all of it. The guitar is the first thing in a long time where I'm genuinely bad, where a twelve year old on YouTube is doing effortlessly what I can't do at all. It's kind of nice honestly. Every week something that was impossible becomes just hard. I practice most nights with headphones so my neighbors don't have to hear the F chord. Ask me in a year how it's going.
