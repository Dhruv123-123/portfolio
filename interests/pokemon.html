Pokémon

Pokémon · since 2013

Pokémon is by far my favorite video game and media franchise of all time. I got into it in 2013 when a family friend gave my brother and me a black Game Boy Advance SP with Pokémon Sapphire and FireRed, and we played those games to death. On our very first playthrough we picked Mudkip, evolved it into Swampert, and then kept leveling it all the way to 83, which is a completely unnecessary level for anything in that game. It brings back good memories.

I've played every single generation of Pokémon game since. Sapphire is still my favorite and Pearl is a close second. Swampert is my favorite Pokémon and given how it all started it was never going to be anything else.

I still have the collection and I keep growing it. Every new game gets played the week it comes out, and I pull out the old cartridges more than I'd probably admit.

I've also played competitively. VGC is the doubles format, and team building matters as much as the actual battles do. I've been ranked as high as 400th on the global leaderboard, and I top cut a regional, finishing 6th in Seniors at the 2023 Fresno Regional. The team is below.

Chi-Yu and Flutter Mane did most of the damage, Amoonguss and Iron Hands kept them alive, Gyarados was there for Intimidate and Thunder Wave, and Baxcalibur cleaned up.

There's a Swampert figure on my desk. When the next Hoenn remake comes out I'll be there day one and I'll pick Mudkip again.
